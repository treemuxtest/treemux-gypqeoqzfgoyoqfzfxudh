Chain Burst is an instant-play arcade toy where the screen is already alive the moment the page appears, and every click detonates a shockwave through a swarm of kinetic orbs. The entire experience is tuned for raw game feel: chain reactions, particle explosions on impact, heavy-but-snappy screen shake, responsive audio pops, and a score counter that physically bounces as you rack up combos. It stays endlessly replayable by feeding new orbs from the edges and letting your timing create emergent cascades, not scripted levels. Despite the polish, it is fully self-contained in a single Next.js page with a tight Canvas loop and zero backend or external assets.
